Payments

Package and service fees are agreed individually before any service begins, based on the exact services and level of support you need, and are confirmed following your free discovery call. Where agreed, monthly coaching is billed in advance on a recurring basis, and any block bookings (for example 3-month or 6-month bundles) are paid in advance. Cancellation Policy

Monthly coaching packages: there are no lock-in contracts. You may cancel your ongoing monthly package at any time by giving 30 days' written notice (by email to coach@kmarfitness.co.uk). Your package remains active, and is payable, until the end of that notice period.

Individual sessions (in-person PT, virtual PT and sports massage): if you need to reschedule or cancel a booked session, please give at least 24 hours' notice. Sessions cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice, or missed without notice, may be charged in full, as the time has been reserved for you.

Refund Policy

Because coaching and training are personalised services delivered over time, fees for sessions already delivered are non-refundable. For prepaid block bookings, any sessions you have not yet used may be refunded on a pro-rata basis, less any discount that was applied to the block, at Kmar Fitness's reasonable discretion. Sports massage appointments that have been attended are non-refundable.
