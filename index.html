What Is kmarfitness?

kmarfitness is a professional personal training, online coaching and sports massage service based in Lymington, Hampshire. Founded by Kayleigh Aitken-Robinson — a Level 3 qualified personal trainer, certified online coach and sports massage therapist — kmarfitness provides beginner-friendly, inclusive, evidence-based coaching for women and men of all ages and abilities across the New Forest and online throughout the UK.

kmarfitness specialises in online coaching, 1-to-1 personal training, sports massage therapy, fat loss coaching, women's fitness coaching, strength training, lifestyle coaching and beginner fitness coaching. In-person sessions serve Lymington, New Milton, Milford on Sea, Pennington, Brockenhurst, Sway, Lyndhurst and the wider New Forest area.

Quick Answer

Kayleigh Aitken-Robinson is a Level 3 qualified personal trainer, certified online coach and sports massage therapist based in Lymington, Hampshire. She is the founder of kmarfitness and specialises in beginner-friendly, evidence-based fitness coaching for women and men across the New Forest and online throughout the UK.

Hi, I'm Kayleigh — a qualified personal trainer, online coach and sports massage therapist based in Lymington, Hampshire. I founded kmarfitness because I know how intimidating fitness can feel, especially when you're just starting out or trying to rebuild your confidence. I wanted to create something that felt supportive, welcoming and realistic for everyday people — in Lymington, across the New Forest and beyond.

Before moving into fitness, I worked as a teaching assistant — and that experience shaped the way I coach today. I'm patient, encouraging and focused on helping people feel comfortable enough to grow at their own pace.

Whether your goal is fat loss, building strength, improving fitness, boosting confidence or simply feeling better in yourself — I'll build a personalised programme around your lifestyle, not take it over. No pressure. No judgement. Just genuine support and coaching that actually works for you.

Fat loss coaching at kmarfitness takes an evidence-based, whole-person approach — combining personalised training programmes, practical nutrition guidance and sustainable lifestyle habits. Unlike a restrictive diet, coaching focuses on building behaviours that last long after the programme ends. The goal is sustainable fat loss that fits your real life, not a crash approach that leaves you back to square one.
For most people, online coaching with a qualified personal trainer delivers far more value than a gym membership alone. A gym gives you access to equipment — a coach gives you a plan, accountability, expert guidance, regular check-ins and someone in your corner when motivation dips. kmarfitness online coaching is flexible, personal and designed around your life, not a gym's timetable.
Sports massage at kmarfitness involves a qualified therapist using targeted techniques to relieve muscle tension, reduce soreness and support recovery. Sessions typically last 60 minutes and are tailored to your specific needs — whether you're recovering from a hard training week, managing a chronic tightness, or simply looking after your body. Sports massage is suitable for athletes and non-athletes alike.
As a beginner, 2–3 training sessions per week is the ideal starting point. This gives your body time to adapt and recover while building consistent habits. Kayleigh will design your programme to match your current fitness level, schedule and goals — gradually increasing intensity and frequency as you get stronger and more confident. Quality and consistency always beat quantity.
