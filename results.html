Sustainable Fat Loss
for Busy Beginners

👩 Online Coaching Beginner-friendly Flexible schedule Home or gym

A common goal at kmarfitness is losing weight in a way that actually lasts. For a busy beginner juggling work and family, that means a plan that fits real life — not an extreme diet or hours in the gym. Coaching focuses on manageable, sustainable changes built around your schedule.

Everything starts with a free discovery call. From there, Kayleigh builds a personalised training and nutrition plan with regular check-ins, so progress stays realistic and you're supported at every step — without pressure or judgement.

Complete Wellness Package

Training and Recovery, Combined

For those managing everyday aches or wanting to recover well between sessions, the Complete Wellness approach pairs personal training with sports massage therapy. Working with one qualified practitioner means your training and recovery are joined up, supporting movement and overall wellbeing.

Virtual PT Sessions

A Safe, Gradual Return to Fitness

Returning to exercise after a break, injury or operation calls for patience and a careful plan. Virtual PT sessions let Kayleigh guide you through progressive, appropriately paced training from home, always mindful of your starting point. Always follow the guidance of your GP or physiotherapist before returning to exercise.

🏃 Rebuild gradually 🖥 Guided online At your own pace

Youth Coaching

Building Confidence in Young People

Drawing on Kayleigh's background as a teaching assistant, youth coaching focuses on confidence, movement skills and healthy habits in an encouraging, age-appropriate way. Kayleigh is DBS checked and all sessions with under-18s follow strict safeguarding guidelines, with parents kept informed.
