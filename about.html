My Story

From Teaching Assistant
to Personal Trainer

Hi, I'm Kayleigh — and I'm really glad you're here. Before I became a personal trainer, I worked as a teaching assistant. That might seem like an unusual path into fitness, but it's shaped everything about how I coach.

Working with young people — especially those who needed extra patience, encouragement and a safe space to grow — taught me more about human potential than any textbook could. The skills I built there: listening carefully, celebrating small wins, never comparing someone's progress to anyone else's — those are the foundations of every coaching relationship I build at kmarfitness.

I founded kmarfitness because I saw too many people give up on fitness not because they lacked commitment, but because they weren't given the right support. Fitness can be genuinely intimidating — particularly if you're a beginner, if you've had a bad experience before, or if you've spent years feeling like fitness "isn't for people like you". I wanted to change that.

Today, I work with clients across Lymington, the New Forest and online throughout the UK. Some are athletes. Most are everyday people who simply want to feel stronger, healthier and more confident. Every single one of them deserves excellent coaching — and that's what I'm here to provide.

How I Coach

My Coaching Philosophy

Three principles that guide every coaching relationship, every programme and every session at kmarfitness.

01

Evidence-Based Methods

Everything at kmarfitness is grounded in exercise science and proven methodology — not trends, fads or shortcuts. Kayleigh's approach uses progressive overload, practical nutrition guidance and sustainable habit-building to deliver real, lasting results. No gimmicks. No quick fixes. Just what actually works.

02

Genuine Care for the Individual

No two clients are the same. Before building any programme, Kayleigh takes time to truly understand your goals, your lifestyle, your history and your mindset. The programme that works for you is built around your life — not a template. That personal investment is what separates kmarfitness from a generic gym programme.

03

Sustainable Habits Over Short-Term Results

Short-term results are easy to produce and hard to keep. kmarfitness coaching focuses on building the habits, behaviours and mindset that sustain results long after the programme ends. Fitness should enhance your life — not dominate it. The goal is always a healthier, more confident you that lasts.
