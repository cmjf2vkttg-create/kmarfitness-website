Recovery & Therapy

Sports Massage
& Recovery

What is it?

Sports massage at kmarfitness is professional therapeutic massage delivered by Kayleigh Aitken-Robinson, a VTCT-qualified sports massage therapist in Lymington. It relieves muscle tension, accelerates recovery and improves performance. Suitable for athletes and non-athletes alike.

Sports massage is not just for elite athletes. Anyone who trains, sits at a desk, carries tension or manages chronic muscle tightness can benefit. Kayleigh uses targeted pressure techniques to release adhesions, restore range of motion and support your body's natural recovery.

Qualified VTCT sports massage therapist based in Lymington
60-minute sessions tailored to your specific needs
Effective for back pain, sports recovery and desk-related tension
Reduces DOMS and accelerates recovery between training sessions
Suitable for all — you don't need to be an athlete
Available as a standalone service or within the Complete Wellness package
