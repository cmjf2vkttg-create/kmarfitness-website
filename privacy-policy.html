Privacy Policy

How Kmar Fitness collects, uses and protects your personal information.

Who We Are

Kmar Fitness provides personal training, online coaching and sports massage services. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information responsibly and in accordance with UK data protection legislation.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Information submitted through enquiry forms
  • Health and fitness information voluntarily provided by you
  • Website usage information collected through Google Analytics

How We Use Your Information

Your information may be used to:

  • Respond to enquiries
  • Provide coaching services
  • Arrange consultations and discovery calls
  • Improve our website and services
  • Communicate with you regarding your coaching programme

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to help understand how visitors interact with our website. Analytics cookies are only activated once consent has been provided through our cookie banner.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use Silktide Consent Manager and Google Consent Mode v2 to manage cookie preferences. Analytics and marketing technologies only collect data after consent has been given.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or destruction.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. You also have the right to withdraw consent where applicable.
